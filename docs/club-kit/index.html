Your clubs at a glance

A card for each of your clubs sits at the top of every page. Fold it away with one click when you want the room.

  • Star rating, coloured by level, with the men's average
  • Junior-league status: are you on track to climb?
  • TP bar, plus roughly when to train the bench and the full team, with a ⚡ when they're ready
  • Tier watch: how many stars until your club moves up (or down) a star tier
  • Lineup check: a warning if a weak man is pitching while a real pitcher sits
  • Good-match and BigLeague records, today, streak, last 10
  • A season clock: when the current season closes

Your record in every stadium

Open your club's page and every stadium card shows how you've done there: home and away, win percentage and runs a game. The parks you don't own get cards too, even at 0–0.

  • This season or all seasons
  • All games or good matches only
  • Other parks as cards, a compact list, or hidden

Your season, three ways

The long "This season's games" list gets three views: Summary, Last 10 or the Full season. The kit remembers your pick.

The summary sums up games, record and win percentage, good matches, today, runs a game, streak and the last ten, plus the clubs you've played most and your record against each.

Your squad, sharpened

The squad table gets Skills, Stats and All buttons, so ratings and batting numbers stop fighting for room.

  • Every skill star coloured by level, the same colours as club stars
  • The long Overall star row becomes one coloured number
  • On your own clubs: each man's TP bar and when he'll be full
  • Click any column header to sort, high to low or low to high
  • Mood gets a face: 😄 🙂 😐 🙁 😣
  • A Pitching view: every pitcher's season line, on any club
  • A Compare view: two date ranges side by side, to see how training helped
  • Training on your own clubs: a Skill board and a report on every session (below)

Did that training work?

The Training button on your own clubs gives every session a report card: what it cost, what it bought, and whether it showed up in games.

  • TP used and skill points gained, with points per 100 TP
  • Each skill checked against the stats it should move: Catching → errors and outs, Bat Power → homers and extra-base hits, Contact → strikeouts and average, and so on
  • Before vs after over the same number of games, with a plain verdict: Showing, Leaning, No clear change or Too early
  • It only says Showing once a change beats normal game-to-game swing, so one hot week can't fool you
  • Quick Trains are spotted too, from the TP they drain

Brand-new club? Still the full picture

No games yet, no problem. Training opens with a Skill board: every man and every skill as a color grid, starters first, with the last session's skills outlined, a small + or − on every skill for the last 24 hours, and each man's TP alongside.

  • Colors scale to the club's best skill, so a young club's strengths still stand out
  • Sessions show their gains right away; the game checks fill in once games are played
  • Missed a session? The site's own record of it still shows: the skill, about when, and about how much TP
  • Skill progress charts track the club's average in each skill as it grows

Pitching lines and side-by-side ranges

Pitching shows every pitcher's season, on any club: games, won–lost, innings, runs allowed per 9, WHIP, hits, walks, strikeouts and homers per 9, complete games and shutouts. SmallBall's pages don't show these.

Compare puts two ranges side by side, like since your last training vs the one before, the last 7 days vs the 7 before, or any dates you pick, for the club, every batter and every pitcher.

Team notes

A sticky-note board on every club page, yours and your rivals'. Lineup ideas, who to train next, opponents to watch.

  • Saves as you type, separately for each club
  • Pin a note to the front, change its color, or copy them all
  • Kept in your browser, just for you

📻 SmallBall Radio

Turn on the radio for any game you watch in SmallBall's own player. Mack Ramsey calls it (a shameless homer for the home club) and Dewey Seeds brings the color, with a guest who walks in after the first half.

  • Every hit, walk, strikeout and run the moment the player shows it, and the score at every change of inning
  • Stories from the old days, SmallBall Trivia, Around the BigLeague and the seventh-inning stretch on the organ
  • No spoilers and no dead air: the booth only talks about what's on screen, and fills the quiet between plays
  • Pick the voices, the guests, how chatty it gets and how often you hear the score. Your browser's own speech; nothing is sent anywhere

And the little things

Quick tabs

Big one-click buttons for your clubs beside the selector, each with its ID. The gear picks which clubs show, in your order.

Your own logos

Add any image on your club page. It's fitted to the same square and shown on your tabs, cards and club header. Only you see it.

Readable selector

The club dropdown follows your theme, so it's never white on white, and lists the clubs you use most at the top.

Ready alerts

When a club's bench or full team is full of TP, your browser tab's title starts with ⚡, so you can spot it from any tab.

Turn anything off

The gear menu lists every feature with a switch. Untick what you don't want and the page reloads without it.

On your phone

Phone browsers can't load extensions, so the Club Kit also comes as one script file that a free app runs for you. The phone edition is built for a quick look: just the big picture, no heavy lifting.

  • A card for each of your clubs: stars and tier, Junior status, a TP bar with when to train the bench and full team, Good matches, today's games and the streak
  • ▶ Play picks that club and opens SmallBall's Games page. You choose the opponent and press Play there
  • Training opens that club's training page on SmallBall
  • The lineup check and the "ready to train" alert, so nothing slips by
  • Quick tabs and the season clock. The box-score tools stay on the computer

iPhone, in Safari

  1. Install Userscripts from the App Store (free) and open it once.
  2. Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → Userscripts: on.
  3. Save the phone script into the Userscripts folder in Files. The name must end in .user.js.
  4. On play.smallball.com tap aA → Userscripts → Always Allow on This Website, then reload.

Android, in Firefox

  1. Install Firefox, then add Tampermonkey from its add-ons menu.
  2. Download the phone script.
  3. Tampermonkey dashboard → Utilities → Import from file, and pick it.
  4. Open play.smallball.com in Firefox.

Get it

  1. Download the zipOr DM slipnsliders on Discord for the latest version.
  2. Unzip itSomewhere you'll keep it. The folder holds manifest.json.
  3. Open chrome://extensionsTurn on Developer mode, top right.
  4. Load unpackedPick the folder, then refresh play.smallball.com.

Plays fair

It's read-only. It never plays games, trains, changes lineups or saves anything for you. It only reads your own clubs' pages, the same ones you can open yourself.

Logos and settings stay in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
