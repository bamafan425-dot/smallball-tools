Tournament Kit

Run a SmallBall tournament from a Google Form sign-up. The kit pulls the sign-ups, builds the bracket, reads results from the real games between the two clubs and keeps the bracket spoiler-safe.

Eight formats

Single elimination

Lose once and you're out. Byes go to the top seeds.

Double elimination

Winners and losers brackets, with an optional bracket reset in the grand final.

Round robin

Everybody plays everybody. Ties go to head to head, then run difference.

Groups, then knockout

Round-robin groups, then the top of each group into a bracket.

Swiss

Nobody's knocked out. Each round pairs clubs with the same record, no rematches.

King of the Hill

The top seed holds the hill and every other club gets one shot, in seed order.

The Gauntlet

The bottom seeds play first and the winner climbs, seed by seed, to the top seed in the final.

Survivor

Single elimination, with the draw redone at random every round.

How it runs

📝 Sign-ups

Publish your Google Form's responses sheet as CSV and paste the link. Every club is checked on SmallBall.

⚾ Results

The kit finds the games between the two clubs and moves the winner on, about every 45 seconds while SmallBall is open. Settle or undo any match by hand.

🙈 Spoiler-safe

A result stays hidden until its game has been watched in the Broadcast Kit.

📣 Share

A Discord-ready post with who's up, results so far and the seeds, plus a bracket code anyone with the kit can load.

🎲 Options

Best of 1, 3, 5 or 7, seeding by sign-up order, stars or random, and Park Roulette for exhibitions.

Install

  1. Download the zip and unzip it somewhere you'll keep it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (Chrome, Edge or Brave) and turn on Developer mode, top right.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder, then refresh play.smallball.com.
  4. Updating: unzip the new version over the old folder and click ↻ reload on the kit in chrome://extensions.

A gold 🏆 Tournament button sits above the red 📺 Broadcast button. Install the Broadcast Kit too for the watch-along.
