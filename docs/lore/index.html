Timeline

  1. 1999SmallBall Baseball launches as a Java game on RealArcade. Today's ball physics are ported straight from it.
  2. Feb 2001Season 1 opens (Feb 18). A new season every two weeks for the next 18 and a half years.
  3. 2002The boom. 120,439 sign-ups and owners in 30+ countries. The Deluxe Trainer arrives, the Eighth League is added, and the 900 Club sells clubs #900–999. The forums open in October.
  4. 200369,420 clubs are born, the biggest crop ever. Homerun Park opens.
  5. 2004Letterbox Park is retired. On Fat Tuesday the forums go down and owners write poetry about it.
  6. Fall 2004Season 100: a free DT for everyone, the Season 100 badge, and Centennial Park.
  7. 2006Commish goes quiet. Slip'N'Sliders opens The Search for Commish (Aug 10). Greek Wizard resigns, the official chat closes in October, and the original forums close for good on New Year's Eve (The Farewell).
  8. Aug 2007The Commish Returns (Aug 16), with a record 39 people in chat.
  9. 2009Old players drift back to the forums, crediting Commish being back and active.
  10. 2019Season 483 ends that summer and the lights go out.
  11. Nov 2026Season 484 opens (Nov 20). 242,191 clubs and 2,906,292 men come back with their old skills.
  12. Nov 2026The Black Friday Brawl: 23 clubs, no match clock. Ajax Assassins (N*S) win from the losers bracket; Mr.Crowley is voted MVC.

Ballparks

League field
The default park.
Monster
Originally "Fox Stadium." Rewards speed, gloves and arms.
World
Deep fences, built for contact and speed clubs.
Ocean / Homerun
Pinball-machine scores.
BrickYard
Limits right-handed power. A catch off the bricks isn't an out.
Alice's
The lefties' park, and Alice is very distracting.
Field of Dreams
Plays fair. Watch out for the corn.
Jurassic
The cliffs keep everything in the yard. Mind the dinosaur.
Centennial
The Season 100 park.
Letterbox
Wide and short. Retired in 2004, back today.

Slang

Footy
SmallBall Football (soccer).
PL1, PL2…
ProLeague levels, nine of them, from Junior up to First League.
DT
The Deluxe Trainer, as in "9* DT." DT-envy is wishing you had one.
★ / *
A club's star level. Beware the old man preaching against the 10th.
Pie
Your training time. Whole Pie or Focus Training means spending it all on one skill.
Zz
Asleep. Probably an easy win.
Spawners
Owners who make no-name clubs just to beat them.
Frogging
Using several computers to start several games at once.
Plug & Play
Bragging on the forums, then challenging everyone.
Hit List
The clubs you're coming for.
Ghost
A former owner who still lurks.
Green Team
The forum moderators.
Knuckle dusted
Winning First League, then vanishing.
U.F.O.
Unconfirmed Fraudulent Opponent.
Pulls
Dragging a man across the field to train running.
Leak
A bug that gives out extra TP.
Test Day
Games are played but don't count.
Honor Rating
A measure of the stars of the clubs you've beaten.
700 teams
