The timeline

  1. Oct 2002The forums open. Greek Wizard is member #1, Commissioner #2, C_J #3.
  2. Apr 2006Commish's last forum post (April 11). Nobody knows it yet.
  3. Aug 2006Football goes down and Slip'N'Sliders opens The Search for Commish (Aug 10).
  4. Sep 2006Commish's name flashes on Who's Online, then vanishes (Sep 10). Greek Wizard asks: the game might not be saved, but can the community? Chat closes and the "goodbye everybody" posts start (Sep 23).
  5. Dec 2006The old forums close. Greek Wizard's last post on New Year's Eve says Commish had his chance to respond. The community moves to smallball.org.
  6. Aug 2007The Commish Returns (Aug 16): 39 people in chat, a new record.
  7. Apr 2009Old players come back to the forums, and "Commish being back and active" gets the credit.
  8. 2026The relaunch at play.smallball.com, a Discord, Hold the Hill, and this Lore Vault.

Glossary

Footy
SmallBall Football (soccer). Its going down kicked off the search.
PL1, PL2…
The Pro Leagues. PL1 is the top. "Qual list" means you're about to qualify in.
DT
A team type you'll see everywhere in old signatures, as in "9* DT." (Know the official meaning? Submit it.)
★ / *
A club's star level. 9★ is elite; after the 2007 downtime, nobody had one.
700 teams
Premium teams sold in the SmallBall Mall. Some buyers never got theirs.
AndNow
The company behind SmallBall in the 2000s.
Hockey comes out tomorrow
The running joke since 2006. It is always tomorrow.
BRB, Commish terms
About 2 years (pizzaguy, 2007).
