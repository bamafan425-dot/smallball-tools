The case log

  1. Apr 11, 2006Last known post. Commish posts on the forums, then goes silent.
  2. Aug 10The case opens. Inspired by Footy being down, Slip checks the obvious places, then Google, and turns up a Commish profile on a sports site. He signs up and sends friend requests. No word yet.
  3. Aug 10The phones. Wookie8662 calls the company numbers: busy signals, then "no longer in service." chspogo swears he once heard Commish's voice on an answering machine.
  4. Aug 10Escalation. oaf proposes a SmallBall gathering outside Commish's house, with torches and pitchforks and the whole deal.
  5. Aug 11"FOUND..." amg111 thinks he may have found a way to save SmallBall and is waiting for a phone number by email. It is the last the thread hears of the plan.
  6. Aug 12The confession. pizzaguy: Ok ok ok, I admit, I'm the commish... hferris: That explains the state of Smallball.
  7. Aug 12The boycott. TThomp's plan: stop spending. Grab him by the wallet. Texas floats a theory that the owner disappeared for six months on purpose to see if the game can float.
  8. Aug 14A legend is born. jefeesdios: Hockey is coming out Tomorrow! By September it's in people's signatures. In 2007 jt was still saying it.
  9. Aug 14A real lead. Feldon reports the last word was that Commish took a product development job at Nokia's N-Gage.
  10. Aug 14–17The Great Ultimate Frisbee War. See exhibit C. Nobody finds Commish.
  11. Aug 20Footy is fixed. Slip calls the search semi-successful and asks if it's been long enough to file a missing persons report.
  12. Aug 21The poster. nyyderek2 makes a Commish "missing" poster and hangs it around his neighborhood. The reward gets bid up in the replies.
  13. Sep 10The ghost. Slip spots the infamous black bold letters of Commish in Who's Online. West Coast refreshes and the name is gone. A day later it's off the list again.
  14. Sep 13"He is a tease!" Slip on the drive-by visit. Money adds that Commish hasn't read one of his PMs in four months.
  15. Sep 14The turn. Greek Wizard: the game and the community are two separate things. The game might not be able to be saved, but the community can be. The thread answers yes.
  16. Sep 18Why keep going? When people ask to shut the thread down, Slip answers: Some still have hope.
  17. Sep 19A sighting. jon2s says he located a quote from Commish on a phone site.
  18. Oct 3, 2006Last entry. Someone checks the records: smallball.com is renewed through 2010, but the forum domain expires October 22. The game would outlive its forums.

Exhibit C: The Great Ultimate Frisbee War

Four days into the search, someone asked why SmallBall's next sport should be hockey and not Ultimate Frisbee. Three pages later the forum had cited Wikipedia and dictionary.com, posted highlight videos, compared it to golf, chess, mini-golf, tee-ball, competitive eating and auto racing, and worked out that you can also play soccer with a dog.

bailey settled nothing: S ome P ractice O f R ecreational T raining. Then: way off topic, he kept checking to see if the man had turned up, and everyone was still slapping each other around.

Epilogue

The thread went quiet in October, and Greek Wizard's farewell came next. The old forums closed on New Year's Eve 2006 and the community carried on at smallball.org. Then, one year and six days after Slip opened the case, Commish walked into the chat room and said "hell." Then "hello."
