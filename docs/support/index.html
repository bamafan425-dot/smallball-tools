Claim your perks

  1. Chip in with one of the buttons above. It's a one-time payment; come back any time.
  2. Add a note in PayPal with your SmallBall club ID and Discord name. Forgot? Just DM slipnsliders on Discord.

Where the money goes

Straight answer: the site itself costs nothing to host. Your support pays for the rest.

  • The hours that go into building and fixing the kits
  • The accounts behind the Hold the Hill and tournament scripts
  • Coffee, and treats for the dogs in the pictures

This isn't a charity, so it isn't tax-deductible. SmallBall ToolKits is a fan project and isn't run by SmallBall or the Commish.
