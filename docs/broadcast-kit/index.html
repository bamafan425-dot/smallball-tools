Broadcast Kit

A watch-along multiview for SmallBall: up to four games in SmallBall's own player, started together on a countdown, with the results kept hidden until you've watched.

Download v0.11.0Full guide & source

Read-only · 55 KB · works alongside the Club Kit

What it does

📺 Up to four games

Single, 2×2 or Main + 3. Queue games by number or browse a club's games and add them with one click.

⏱ Synced start

A five-second countdown, then every tile starts at once. 1×, 2× or 4× for all of them together.

🙈 Spoiler shield

Tracked games you haven't watched have their scores blurred around the site, and the kit never stores a score.

🎙 The Booth

Every tile's play-by-play gathered into one live feed, with highlights. Copy or download transcripts for Discord.

📻 SmallBall Radio

Mack Ramsey and Dewey Seeds call the game out loud, with a guest, stories, trivia, the seventh-inning stretch and score updates at every change of inning.

📡 Stream Mode (SBSN)

Turns the broadcast into a TV screen for OBS or StreamYard: score bug, captions, a BigLeague ticker, segment cards and auto-pilot to the next game.

🏆 Tournament hand-off

Follow tournament clubs: their new games join the queue on their own. Works with the Tournament Kit.

Install

  1. Download the zip and unzip it somewhere you'll keep it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (Chrome, Edge or Brave) and turn on Developer mode, top right.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder, then refresh play.smallball.com.
  4. Updating: unzip the new version over the old folder and click ↻ reload on the kit in chrome://extensions.

The red 📺 Broadcast button sits at the bottom left of play.smallball.com.
