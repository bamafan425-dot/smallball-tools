# 🧢 SmallBall Club Kit

A free make-over for play.smallball.com: your clubs, your stars and your records, on every page.
Website with screenshots: **https://slipnsliders.github.io/smallball-tools/club-kit/**

- `extension/` is the browser extension (Chrome, Edge, Brave). `extension/README.txt` has the full feature guide.
- `phone/SmallBall-Club-Kit.user.js` is the phone edition (Safari + Userscripts on iPhone, Firefox + Tampermonkey on Android).

**Download the latest:** [SmallBall-Club-Kit.zip](https://github.com/slipnsliders/smallball-tools/raw/main/downloads/SmallBall-Club-Kit.zip) ·
[phone script](https://github.com/slipnsliders/smallball-tools/raw/main/downloads/SmallBall-Club-Kit.user.js)

`extension/radio.js` is built from the Broadcast Kit's radio booth (`broadcast-kit/tk.js`), so both kits share one radio.
