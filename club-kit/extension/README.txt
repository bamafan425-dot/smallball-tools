SmallBall Club Kit
A little make-over for play.smallball.com. Everything stays in your own browser.

🧪 PLAYEROPTIMIZER III BY JON2S (new in 1.26.0, computer editions)
  Jon2s's lineup optimizer, built in with his blessing. Open one of your clubs and scroll to LINEUP:
  AUTO-OPTIMIZE (blue, next to Save) suggests positions and a 1-9 batting order, with the reasons.
  It fills in the form only: review it, then press Save yourself. Turn it off in ⚙ under Features.
  His own notes are in PlayerOptimizer-III-README.txt. Thanks, Jon2s! 🙌

🔁 ROTATE BENCH (new in 1.26.0, computer editions)
  For Passive Training clubs, next to Save on your lineup editor: games train the men who play and spend
  their TP, so one click swaps up to three bench men with the most TP in for the most drained starters,
  each taking that starter's position. Your pitcher stays, and a swap only happens when the bench man has
  20+ more TP. It fills in the form only; press Save yourself. Turn it off in ⚙ under Features.

🏋️ TRAINER OR QUICK TRAIN? (1.26.0)
  Training Effectiveness now tells the two apart. ⚡ Quick Train: the club's "last trained" time didn't move.
  🏋️ Trainer: it moved to that session (so one skill in the trainer is no longer called a Quick Train).
  Plain "Training" means the kit wasn't reading the club right before and after, so it can't tell.
  It knows for sessions from now on; hover the label for why.

📻 SMALLBALL RADIO (new in 1.25.0, updated in 1.25.1, computer editions)
  Watching a game in SmallBall's own player (any Watch button, or a live game)? A 📻 Radio button sits at
  the bottom left, above 🏟 Skins. Open it and press "Turn the radio on": Mack Ramsey (play-by-play, a
  shameless homer for the home club) and Dewey Seeds (color) call the game out loud, with a guest who
  drops by after the first half. In the breaks you get stories from the old days, SmallBall Trivia,
  Around the BigLeague and the seventh-inning stretch on the booth organ.
  No spoilers: the booth only talks about what the player has already shown on screen. Close the window
  and it keeps talking; the button turns red with ON AIR while it's on. Switch games and it follows.
  Score updates at every change of half-inning, and the booth fills the quiet between plays with the
  situation, who's hot, the pitch count, banter and lore. "📋 How the show goes" in the settings has the rundown.
  ⚙ Booth settings: pick each voice, which guests can visit, The show or straight Play-by-play, how many
  jokes, how many pitches get called, Score updates (every half, every inning, off), Situation
  (inning, outs and score before every batter), Dead air (Some quiet, Steady, Nonstop), Mack (home-team
  homer or down the middle), volume and the organ. "Test the booth" checks your speakers.
  Speech is your browser's own, and nothing is sent anywhere. Skill numbers only come up for your own
  clubs. It's the same booth as the Broadcast Kit's radio and shares its settings; when the Broadcast
  window is open, the Broadcast Kit has the microphone. Turn the button off in ⚙ under Features.

⛰️ REPORT TO HOLD THE HILL (new in 1.24.0)
  On your own club's page, each BigLeague game in the game list has a ⛰️ Report button next to 📰 Box
  score. It opens the Hold the Hill form in a new tab with "Report my game", the game ID, BigLeague and
  both scores already filled in from SmallBall's own game record. Press Next and Submit. Both players
  still report (the form is how the Hill knows it's you). Turn the button off in ⚙ if you don't play.

📰 BOX SCORES (new in 1.23.0)
  SmallBall keeps every game's line score and each player's line but has no box score page, so the kit
  makes one. In SmallBall's game lists every game now shows its game number (#6496) and a 📰 Box score
  button next to Watch. Open one for the inning-by-inning line score with R/H/E, both batting orders (AB, H, 2B, 3B, HR, RBI, BB, K, PO, A, E),
  both pitchers' lines, double plays, the fastest pitch and the highlights. "Copy for Discord" copies a
  neat text box score you can paste straight into the chat.
  Spoiler-safe: a game still on the air shows 🔒 Box score, which only tells you when it unlocks (when
  the report airs). When one of your clubs' games finishes airing (games it started and games it was
  challenged in), a "📰 Box score ready" toast pops up at the bottom right (click it to open), and the tab
  title starts with 📰 until you've opened it. Optional in ⚙: a chime, and a desktop notification (the
  browser asks permission the first time) so you hear about it from another tab. Your clubs are checked
  about every 90 seconds while a SmallBall tab is open, and only one tab alerts. Opponents' names and
  game lines show, since they're public results; nobody's skill numbers do.

GAME COUNTDOWN (timed games)
  Since Nov 24, 2026, SmallBall won't start a club's next official game until its last one has been
  reported, which happens when that game finishes airing. Each club's tab shows ⏳ and the time left
  (⏳5:42), and its card shows "Next game in 5:42" just above ▶ Play. At zero it turns to ✓ play /
  ✅ Ready to play. The clock is the server's, so it matches the site's own "report hasn't aired yet"
  message. When a new game starts, the kit asks SmallBall's own game preview once whether the club is
  really blocked; if it isn't (say, after an exhibition), the tab shows 📺 and the time it stays on the
  air instead. Optional chime when a club can play again: ⚙ → "Chime when a club can play again"
  (off to start). The kit re-checks a club a few seconds after you press the site's own Play button,
  and looks over your clubs every 90 seconds while the page is open. It only reads.
  Only games a club started count (the club that starts a game is the visitor). A game a club was
  challenged in doesn't hold it up, so it gets no countdown; its score is still hidden and its record
  still frozen while it airs if you have those on. Exhibitions never hold anyone up.

HIDE SCORES ON THE AIR (new in 1.20.0, off to start)
  ⚙ → "Hide scores of games still on the air". While a game is still airing, its score in the club's
  game list and in SmallBall's live bar is swapped for its countdown (⏳ 4:12), so you can watch it
  without seeing how it ends. Click the countdown to see the score anyway. When the report airs, the
  score comes back by itself.

FREEZE RECORDS AND STATS (new in 1.22.0, off to start)
  ⚙ → "Freeze records and stats until a game's report airs". SmallBall adds a game's result to the
  club's record the moment it's simulated, about 20 minutes before the game finishes airing. With this
  on, while a game is still on the air its result is taken back out: the club's W-L, games played and
  runs on its page and on Club Kit cards, its row in the BigLeague and Pro League tables, and (if
  SmallBall has already credited them) its players' career lines. The Club Kit's own views (dashboard,
  season games, stadium records, Training Effectiveness and so on) leave the airing game out too. When
  the report airs, everything catches up by itself. Pair it with "Hide scores of games still on the air"
  for a spoiler-free watch. Ranks in the tables aren't re-sorted while a game airs. On the phone script,
  the very first page you load may show the real record; it freezes from your next click on.

What you get
- Quick tabs: big one-click buttons for your clubs, next to the club selector, each with its club ID.
  Press the gear button at the end of the tabs to choose which clubs appear (tick them in the order you want).
  Until you choose, it shows your first four clubs.
- Good matches only: one switch (on the dashboard, the games heading and the squad heading) makes every
  tool count only good matches: dashboard runs/today/streak/last 10, the season summary and games list,
  most-played opponents, stadium records, Pitching and Compare. In the squad's Stats columns it swaps the
  site's career totals for this season's good-match numbers (shown in green), and puts them back when off.
- Settings: the same gear menu has a Features list. Untick anything you don't want; the page reloads.
- A readable club selector: the dropdown list follows your light or dark theme, so it's never white on white,
  and lists the clubs you picked most recently first.
- Club logos: on your own club's page press "Add logo" beside the name and pick any image.
  Each logo is fitted into the same square (centred, never stretched); a plain white background around it
  is made transparent. Logos show on your tabs, your dashboard cards and your club page header. Only you see them.
- Club dashboard at the top of every page: a card per club with its star rating front and centre (ten stars,
  a different colour for every level from bronze to gold, plus the men's average), home stadium,
  Junior-league status (winning good-match record at 5 stars or better), a TP bar with when the starters and bench
  will be full (by design a starter refills in 48 hours and the bench in 24) and roughly when to Quick Train
  the bench and the full team (with a ⚡ when they're ready), how many stars until the next star tier
  (good matches are clubs with the same star rating), good-match and BigLeague records, runs a game,
  today's games, streak and last 10. The header shows when the season closes.
- Ready-to-train alert: when a club's bench or full team is full, the browser tab title starts with ⚡.
- Squad table: on your own club page a TP column shows each man's bar and when he'll be full.
  Player grades color every skill star, and the long Overall star row becomes one colored number
  (same colors as club stars), which also makes the table narrower.
  Skills / Stats / All buttons on "The squad" split the ratings (with Mood) from the batting and fielding stats.
  Pitching (the fourth button) shows each pitcher's season line, worked out from the public box scores:
  games, won-lost, innings, runs allowed per 9, WHIP, hits/walks/strikeouts/homers per 9, complete games,
  shutouts and pitches per game. The first look at a club reads its games (it can take a minute); after
  that they're saved and it's quick.
  Compare (the fifth button) puts two date ranges side by side: since your last training vs the one
  before it, last 3/7/10 days vs the days before, last 25 games vs the 25 before, this season, or any
  dates you pick. It shows win %, runs, each batter's AVG/OBP/SLG/HR/K% and each pitcher's RA/9, WHIP,
  K/9 and BB/9, with the change in green or red. On your own clubs it also spots each training from the
  box scores (when a man's skills jump between games) and shows what each man's skills gained.
  Training (your own clubs) opens with a Skill board: every man and every skill as a color grid (starters
  first, colors scaled to the club's best skill), the last session's skills outlined, and each man's TP.
  A small badge on each cell shows the change in the last 24 hours (green +, amber − if he's forgotten).
  It needs no games, so a brand-new club gets the full picture right away. If the kit missed a session,
  the site's own note of it still shows (skill, about when, about how much TP, where each man is now).
  At the bottom, Skill progress charts the club's average in each skill over time, built from your visits.
  Training (your own clubs) is Training Effectiveness: a card for each training session found in the
  box scores, newest first. It names the session (e.g. "Catching Quick Train"), shows the TP it used and
  the skill points it bought (points per 100 TP), and draws each man's trained skill before, gained and
  forgotten since. Then it checks the stats that skill should move, before vs after:
    Pitching: strikeouts, walks, hits and runs per 9 (the pitchers who trained it)
    Catching: errors per chance, putouts, the club's errors and opponents' average on balls in play
    Throwing: assists, errors per chance, the club's double plays and runs allowed
    Running: doubles + triples per hit, average on balls in play, the club's runs per man on base
    Bat Skill: strikeout %, walk %, on-base %, the club's runs
    Bat Power: home runs and extra-base hits per at-bat, isolated power, the club's runs
    Bat Contact: strikeout %, batting average, average on balls in play, the club's runs
  Each check says Showing, Leaning right, No clear change, Leaning wrong, Went the wrong way or Too
  early to tell. A change only counts as Showing once it's bigger than normal game-to-game swing.
  TP: the site doesn't record what a training used, so the kit notes each man's TP whenever it reads your
  club and works the spend out from the drop. Quick Trains are caught too. Sessions from before you
  installed 1.14 show an estimate (≈) for the latest one only.
  Not enough games yet? The session still shows, as "Gains only": TP used, skill points bought and each
  man's before → after. The game checks fill in once there are 3 games on each side. Clubs with no games
  at all still get their sessions, spotted from your TP and skills between visits to the club page.
  Mood gets a face: 😄 90+, 🙂 70+, 😐 50+, 🙁 30+, 😣 below.
  Click "Your clubs" to fold it away. Refresh to update; otherwise it reuses what it read in the last two minutes.
- Team notes: a sticky-note board on every club page (yours and others), just above The squad. Add as
  many notes as you like; each saves as you type. Pin one to the front, change its color, or tap ✕ twice
  to delete. "Copy all" copies every note for that club. Notes stay in this browser, just for you.
- Passive Training chip (your clubs' dashboard cards): every card shows "Passive ON" (purple) or
  "Passive off" (grey). Click it to jump to SmallBall's own Passive Training switch: it's in the Settings box
  at the very bottom of each club's page, and the kit scrolls there and flashes it so you can flip it
  yourself (the kit never changes it for you). When Passive is on, a line under the chip explains it. Normally it reminds you that every game, good matches included,
  spends the starters' TP. When the starters are out of TP it turns into a warning: games aren't
  training them, so turn Passive off until they refill.
- Lineup check (your clubs' dashboard cards): an amber warning when the man pitching clearly isn't your
  pitcher, e.g. "Derek Thomas (Pitching 5) is pitching. Bear Bryant (Pitching 96) is on the bench."
  It shows when someone on the bench or elsewhere in the field pitches 25+ points better (and 50+).
  Training the bench can move bench men into the lineup, and club stars follow the nine who start.
- Stadium records: in the Stadiums section of your club page, each stadium you own shows your record there
  (home and away, win %, runs a game). Every other stadium is listed too, 0–0 if you haven't played there:
  as matching cards, as a compact text list, or hidden (your choice, remembered).
  Choose this season or all seasons, and all games or good matches only.
  The game list doesn't say where each game was played, so the first time it looks every game up once
  (about 10 a second) and remembers them; after that only new games are looked up.
- Season games, three ways: "This season's games" on club pages has Summary, Last 10 and Full season buttons
  (your pick is remembered). The summary shows games, record and win %, good matches, BigLeague, today,
  runs a game, streak, last 10, and your most-played opponents with your record against each.

What it doesn't do
- It never plays games, trains, changes lineups or saves anything. It only reads your own clubs' pages.

Install (Chrome, Edge or Brave)
1. Unzip this folder somewhere you'll keep it (keep manifest.json inside).
2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
3. Load unpacked -> pick this folder.
4. Refresh play.smallball.com.

PHONE EDITION (SmallBall-Club-Kit-vX.user.js)
  Built for a quick look on your phone, not the full toolbox. It shows a card for each of your clubs
  (stars and tier, Junior status, TP with when to train, Good matches, today's games, streak), the
  lineup check, quick tabs and the season clock. The box-score tools (Pitching, Compare, Training,
  stadium records, squad views, notes) stay on the computer, so it's light on your phone and the site.
  Every dashboard card (phone and computer) has two buttons:
    ▶ Play     picks that club with the site's own club switcher and opens SmallBall's Games page.
               You choose the opponent and press Play there. The kit never starts a game itself.
    Training   opens that club's training page on SmallBall.
  iPhone: Userscripts app (free) in Safari. Android: Tampermonkey in Firefox. See the website for steps.

TABS AND CARDS ARE ONE THING NOW (computer)
  Each quick tab is the header of its club's dashboard card. The tabs sit on their own full-width row
  under SmallBall's header line, three equal columns across. Closed, it's a button showing the star tier,
  ⚡ when the club is ready to train and ⚠ when the card has a warning (lineup or Passive). Open, the
  card hangs off the bottom of the tab, right where it is.
    Click         open or close that club's card
    Double-click  go to the club's full team page
  Every page starts with all the cards closed, so you only see the tabs until you open one. "Your clubs ▾" opens or closes them all. The phone
  works as before: a tap switches club, and the cards stay in a list.

LIVE GAMES BAR
  When your clubs have games live, SmallBall shows a bar of them near the top of the page. The kit adds a
  small control above it: Stack (the site's own look), Row (compact chips in one line that scrolls
  sideways) or Hide (folds it behind a "● 3 live games · show" button). Your choice is remembered; the
  phone starts on Row. Switch it off in ⚙ under "Live games bar".

STADIUM SKINS (computer editions)
  Old SmallBall lore: swap a park's picture for your own. Click the green 🏟 Skins button at the bottom left
  of the page (it sits above the Broadcast and Tournament buttons if you have those). Each park has a card
  showing its size and your current skin. Pick a park and choose an
  image; it's resized to that park's view size in the game player (800×600 for most; Home field 671×561,
  Homerun stadium 700×600, Letterbox Park 800×450, Jurassic Park 714×600) and kept in this browser only.
  Making your own? The free Skin Maker Stencil Pack has a guide and a see-through overlay for every park,
  drawn at its exact size, with home, the bases, the mound, the foul poles, the outfield wall and the
  scoreboard area marked where the game puts them. The next game you open at that park, from Watch, a live game or the Tournament Kit,
  shows your skin. Remove it to go back to the site's own picture, or untick "Show my skins in the game
  player" to switch them all off. The kit ships no skins: you bring your own.
  How it works: the player draws each park from a picture built into its page; the kit's small skins.js
  file hands it your image instead when it loads that park. It changes nothing else and sends nothing.
