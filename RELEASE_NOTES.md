**New in Club Kit 1.25:** 📻 SmallBall Radio in the site's own game player. Mack and Dewey call the game out loud, with score updates at every change of inning, situation updates, stories, trivia and a guest in the booth. No spoilers, no dead air.

**New in Broadcast Kit 0.9.4:** the same radio upgrades (score updates, steady chatter, new booth settings, "How the show goes").

**New in Hold the Hill 3.4:** the Ace gets a crowned spot on top of the Climb, every spot shows who it can charge, and "🔎 Who can I charge?" on the form posts your list in #hill-help.

Install: unzip, open `chrome://extensions`, turn on Developer mode, **Load unpacked**, pick the folder. Updating: unzip over the old folder and click ↻ reload.
